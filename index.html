About

A little about how I work.

I like building software that has a clear job to do — from keeping event attendance organised to making a budget work offline.

I’m a Computer Science student working across mobile apps, web tools and computer-vision research. Most of my learning happens while turning an idea into something I can test.

I break problems into smaller parts, try things, and fix what doesn’t work. I’d rather show a working prototype and explain its limits than call unfinished work finished.
